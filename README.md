# ForgeCoach scenery: pair pictures (`pairs-v1-work` branch)

This branch holds ONLY the pair pack. **pairs-v7-pin** (2026-11-02) holds the **painted-in cells** of the seam grid
that pairs-v7's `pairs.json` names by absolute URL on every cell's `elements`:
`seams/<left>-<right>/s<i>x<j>+<C><L|R>-panorama.webp`, the third colour C (W U B R G, never one of the pair's own)
grown into the heavier half's ground of the cell s<i>x<j>, 3277 x 640, WebP -- L the cell's left half where i >= j,
R its right half where i <= j, 22 pictures per third colour per pair, 660 in all. There is no manifest here: load the
pair pack as `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/jalirkan/forgecoach-scenery@pairs-v7/`.

Why a tag of its own: 48.3 MB of pictures, which fit jsDelivr's 50 MB per ref only alone.

Outside a feathered ring around the painted feature every picture is pixel-identical to its pairs-v6 cell; the seam core
(u 0.43-0.57) is never touched, and the half's own sun or moon is held. The motifs are the desktop set's (pairs-v5):
W a shaft of light on stones or a pale stone path, U a cold stream with rapids, B a black bog channel, slick or dead
snags over black pools, R a volcanic sea stack or a dark crag with ember seams and smoke, G an islet grove, a savannah
grove or moss -- each at the half's own stage, hour and horizon.

This tag: 660 pictures, **48,299,848 bytes**, plus this README, PROVENANCE.txt and LICENSE.
