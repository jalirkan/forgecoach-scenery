Scenery pieces, pieces-v1 -- provenance (spec 1.14 `pieces`: elements, fronts, veils, the empty table; named by
pack-v9.2's scenery.json with absolute URLs at this tag)
Painted 2026-10-23 on Justin's PC (GTX 1080) by panoramas-v1's tools (~/scenery-packs/panoramas-v1: tools/b3f.py,
b3fin.py, pano_gen.py; SUMMARY.md there, sections "Batch 3 sketches" and "Batch 3 finished").

INPUTS
- The only image inputs are ForgeCoach's own pack pictures -- pack-v9's pair panoramas from panoramas-v1 (the bases the
  fronts and veils are painted over, and the composites on the sheet) and the batch-3 sketches (the empty table's guide)
  -- and guides painted in code (b3f.py: a neutral grey ground, the elements' and the weathers' silhouettes). No web
  image, no card image, no private file, nothing of Wizards' art as an input or a reference. Each piece is written in
  our own words from SYSTEM.md's slot table.

MODELS AND PIPELINE
- DreamShaper XL 1.0 fp16 (Lykon/dreamshaper-xl-1-0) with madebyollin/sdxl-vae-fp16-fix, no LoRA, DPM++ 2M Karras.
  Nothing downloaded for this tag.
- pano_gen.py: MultiDiffusion img2img over the code-painted guide, one latent, ~1 MP windows; 30-34 scheduled steps at
  0.55-0.85. Elements keyed off a neutral ground (b3fin.py key); fronts and veils pulled against their own colour.
- Every GPU step under flock ~/scenery-packs/logs/gpu.lock with the 6 GB free-memory gate and free -g logged; 81
  pictures, ~2.5 h of generation (~3.0 h of job time with model loads and prompt encodes).
