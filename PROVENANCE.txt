Pair panoramas, pairs-v5 -- provenance (spec 1.16.1 sided painted-in elements over pairs-v4's whole-half panoramas;
the element phone pictures at pairs-v5-phone, the base phones at pairs-v4, the halves and frames at pairs-v2)
Painted 2026-10-24 on Justin's PC (GTX 1080) by panoramas-v1's tools/pin.py (~/scenery-packs/panoramas-v1; SUMMARY.md
there, sections "Painted-in elements (10-24)", "Painted-in stage 2 result", "Painted-in stage 3 result" and "Painted-in
phones result"); tagged 2026-10-24.

INPUTS
- The only image inputs are ForgeCoach's own pack pictures: the finished stage panoramas of pairs-v4 (the cleaned
  4588 x 896 paintings its files were cut from), with each feature roughed in by code as the guide. No web image, no
  card image, no private file, nothing of Wizards' art as an input or a reference. Each feature is written in our own
  words.

MODELS AND PIPELINE
- DreamShaper XL 1.0 fp16 (Lykon/dreamshaper-xl-1-0) with madebyollin/sdxl-vae-fp16-fix, no LoRA, DPM++ 2M Karras.
  Nothing downloaded for this pack.
- pin.py: MultiDiffusion inpainting over a code-painted guide on the finished stage panorama, a 1728 x 896 crop under
  two 1152 x 896 windows; strength 0.85 over 32 scheduled steps; strength map 1 over the feature, 0.4 over a ring, 0
  beyond. The feature on one side's ground (left u 0.19-0.47, right u 0.53-0.81), at the scene's hour and light key.
- Finish: feathered composite onto the base, grain matched to the base's high-pass level, 3277 x 640 Lanczos, WebP q80.
  Outside the crop the picture is pixel-identical to the base (max |diff| 0.0 before encoding).
- A 'moss' motif (moss and ferns over the ground; vines on dead trees on the fen) for three green slots on dark ground
  where trees kept painting as lime: island+mountain s2 GR and s3 GR, mountain+swamp s3 GR.
- Every GPU step under flock ~/scenery-packs/logs/gpu.lock with the 6 GB gate; ~320 pictures (desktop and phones) and
  ~16.5 h of generation for the run. The picks and their verdicts are panoramas-v1/work/pin/picks.json and
  verdicts.json.
