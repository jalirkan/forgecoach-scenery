Pair panoramas, pairs-v4 -- provenance (whole-half pair pictures, spec 1.15; pairs-v2's halves and frames referenced at
their own tag)
Painted 2026-10-22 on Justin's PC (GTX 1080) by panoramas-v1's tools (~/scenery-packs/panoramas-v1: tools/pano.py,
pano_gen.py, stage.py, trim.py; SUMMARY.md there, sections "Batch 1", "Batch 2" and "Trim"); tagged 2026-10-23.

INPUTS
- The only image inputs are ForgeCoach's own pack pictures: pack-v9's stage-5 half pictures and pairs-v2's stage-3 pair
  pictures (forgecoach-scenery tags pack-v9 and pairs-v2), cut into texture plates and laid out with painted silhouettes
  in code (pano.py) as each picture's guide. No web image, no card image, no private file, nothing of Wizards' art as an
  input or a reference. Each place is written from its pair's two biomes in our own words.

MODELS AND PIPELINE
- DreamShaper XL 1.0 fp16 (Lykon/dreamshaper-xl-1-0) with madebyollin/sdxl-vae-fp16-fix, no LoRA, DPM++ 2M Karras.
  Nothing downloaded for this pack.
- pano_gen.py: MultiDiffusion img2img over the code-painted guide, 4608 x 896, seven 1152 x 896 windows with per-window
  prompts, one latent; strength ~0.70-0.80. Stage 3 first (batch 1: four pairs; batch 2: six more and Godless Shrine's
  marble shrine), then stages 1-2 repainted over each stage-3 pick at 0.52 / 0.48, then the 1.28:1 phone pictures.
- Finish: centre crop to 4588 x 896, two model signatures painted out; horizons, hours and keys measured off each picture
  (~/scenery-packs/light.json).
- Trim (CPU, trim.py, candidate D-P1): re-encoded from the cleaned pictures, desktop 3277 x 640 WebP q80, phone
  1638 x 1280 WebP q80, no 2x files.
- Every GPU step under flock ~/scenery-packs/logs/gpu.lock with the 6 GB gate; ~9.2 GPU-hours for batches 1-2.
- The picks and their reasons are panoramas-v1/picks.json and picks-why.json.
