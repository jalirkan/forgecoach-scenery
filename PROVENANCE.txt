Scenes, scenes-v1 -- provenance (scenery spec 1.20: one whole-half painting per exact set of lands, desktop and phone)
Painted 2026-11-06 on Justin's PC (GTX 1080) in the scenes-v1 round (~/scenery-packs/scenes-v1, STATUS.txt and
PLACES.md there). Tagged 2026-11-06 as scenes-v1: eight scenes (the owner's two boards of 2026-11-06 and six from the first draft round).
Tagged 2026-11-06 as scenes-v1.1: 48 scenes, the eight plus the Nile delta region (island+swamp+plains led by plains and
swamp): its hero developed (full size with the place's life, the draft as reference, then Z-Image-Turbo 0.30 for the oil
hand), its phone hero painted upright from it, and every board the region owns (40) as the hero re-rendered by FLUX.2
klein img2img at strength 0.75 with that board's own description, desktop from the desktop hero and phone from the phone
hero; Real-ESRGAN x2 and the finished sizes as before.

INPUTS
- The only image inputs are ForgeCoach's own pack pictures: pack v10's oil monos were given to the painter as reference
  pictures for a few drafts (the "ar" candidates, none of which was picked), and each scene's own desktop painting is the
  reference for its phone painting. No web image, no card image, no private file, nothing of Wizards' art as an input
  or a reference. The words are oil-v2's prompt vocabulary (the five peoples, each land's thing at each stage, the hour
  by the heaviest colour's stage), composed per scene as a place (PLACES.md).

MODELS AND PIPELINE
- FLUX.2 klein 4B (Black Forest Labs, Apache-2.0), text to image, 4 steps, 1792 x 560 for the desktop painting and
  768 x 960 upright for the phone painting (the desktop painting as its reference image); several compositions and
  seeds a scene, picked by eye (the owner's picks for the first two scenes, the lead's for the rest).
- Real-ESRGAN x2 (RealESRGAN_x2plus), then the finished sizes: half 2048 x 640 and 4096 x 1280, phone 1024 x 1280 and
  1536 x 1920, each the upscale with a quarter of the plain Lanczos enlargement mixed back; WebP quality 80.
- Horizon and subject band measured over the middle of each picture (tools/pack_scene.py); hour and key from the
  heaviest colour's stage.
